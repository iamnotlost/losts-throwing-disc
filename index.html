Lost's Throwing Disc

Adds the Throwing Disc to your Paper server. Throw it, it bounces off walls and comes back to you. Five tiers, and only one of each can exist on the server.

What it does

Right-click to throw. No cooldown, no durability, no enchants.

Flies straight

Goes where your crosshair is. No gravity, no drop, 20 blocks out.

Goes through mobs

Hits everything in a line, once going out and once coming back. Damage ignores armor.

Bounces off walls

Comes off the wall at the same angle it hit. Each bounce makes it faster and adds 6 blocks of range.

Comes back

Flies straight back to the hand you threw it from. If your inventory is full, it spins around you until there's space.

The discs

Netherite hits the hardest and doesn't burn in lava. Golden is the fastest.

Crafting

Four of the material in a plus shape. Netherite is an upgrade from diamond at a smithing table.

  • One of each per serverOnce someone crafts the copper disc, nobody else can. Server owners can raise the limit or make it unlimited.
  • Lost discs free upIf a disc burns in lava, falls in the void or despawns, that tier can be crafted again.
  • No dupingShift-click can't make two, crafters can't make them, and anvils don't work on them.

Setup

Put the jar in your plugins folder and restart. The plugin sends its own resource pack when players join, so nobody needs mods.

# plugins/ThrowingDisc/config.yml
disc:
  range: 20.0          # blocks before it turns back
  bounce-bonus: 6.0    # extra blocks per bounce
  max-bounces: 6
tiers:
  netherite: { damage: 12.0, speed: 1.35 }
resource-pack:
  port: 8166
  address: "your.server.ip"   # needed on hosted servers

FAQ

Is this the real vanilla disc?

No, it's a recreation made from what Mojang has shown. The real one might work a bit differently when it comes out.

What versions does it work on?

Paper 26.2 with Java 25. Players on older versions can join with ViaVersion, but they won't see the disc textures.

The textures don't show up

On a hosted server, set resource-pack.address to your server's IP and give the plugin its own open port. If another plugin already hosts a pack on that port, pick a different one.

Can I allow more than one of each?

Yes. /disc limit 3 allows three of every tier, and /disc limit infinite removes the limit.
